Tile Grid Map Pro — Regional maps in Power BI without the GIS headache

On a real map, area lies. Madrid is a dot, Castilla is a continent — and
your data disappears behind the geography. Tile Grid Map Pro gives every
region the same visual weight, so small dense areas are just as readable
as large empty ones.

Drop in latitude and longitude, add a measure, pick your country. That's
the whole setup. 27 countries and regions are built in, and with Pro you
can load any TopoJSON boundary file by dragging it straight onto the
visual — provinces, sales territories, districts, whatever your business
actually runs on.

⬇️ Get it on Microsoft AppSource:
https://appsource.microsoft.com/

✅ WHAT'S IN THE FREE VERSION

• 27 built-in country and region grids — Spain, Canarias, Portugal,
  France, Germany, Italy, Sicily, Sardinia, UK, Netherlands, Belgium,
  Poland, Sweden, Norway, Switzerland, Austria, US, Alaska, Hawaii,
  Canada, Mexico, Brazil, Argentina, Colombia, Australia, India, Japan
• Up to 500 data points
• Five aggregations, auto-detected from your field metadata
• Conditional formatting — three rules including between-range
• Tooltips with up to 10 extra measures
• Cross-filter, Ctrl multi-select, context menu
• High contrast and accessibility options

⭐ WHAT PRO ADDS

• Unlimited data points, up to 30,000 rows
• Custom TopoJSON — drag any boundary file onto the visual
• Boundaries persist inside the .pbix, no re-upload after reopening
• Load TopoJSON from a public HTTPS URL
• Your own colour palettes: sequential, diverging (min/mid/max)
  and categorical
