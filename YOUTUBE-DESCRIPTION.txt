TÍTULO:
How to Build a Tile Grid Map in Power BI (No Map Service Needed) — Tile Grid Map Pro

DESCRIPCIÓN:

Build a tile grid map in Power BI from nothing more than latitude and longitude. Tile Grid Map Pro places every row on a grid cell of the country you choose and colours each cell by your measure — no map service, no shapefiles, no network calls.

In this video: setting it up from scratch, how rows are placed and aggregated, tile shapes, sizing tiles by a second measure, quantile, diverging and categorical colour scales, colour rules with their own legend, keyboard navigation and loading your own TopoJSON regions.

✅ FREE

• 27 built-in maps: Spain, Canarias, Portugal, France, Germany, Italy, Sicily, Sardinia, United Kingdom, Netherlands, Belgium, Poland, Sweden, Norway, Switzerland, Austria, United States, Alaska, Hawaii, Canada, Mexico, Brazil, Argentina, Colombia, Australia, India, Japan
• The first 500 rows of data
• Square tiles, latitude distortion correction
• Coastal points snapped to the nearest land tile; rows outside the map counted on screen
• Aggregation that follows your field: Auto, Sum, Average, Count, Min, Max
• Colour rules (3 rules) with a legend that lists them
• Legend font, size and colour; labels, borders, no-data colour
• Tooltips with up to 10 extra fields
• Cross-filter, Ctrl multi-select, context menu, keyboard navigation, high contrast

⭐ PRO ADDS

• All rows up to 30,000 (the most Power BI sends to a visual)
• Circle and hexagon tiles
• Size — tile area from a second measure
• Quantile, diverging (neutral at zero) and categorical colour scales (by a Category field, with legend)
• Your own scale colours
• Custom TopoJSON regions by drag & drop, saved inside the report
